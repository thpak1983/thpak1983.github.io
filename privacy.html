Privacy Policy

This policy covers the private personal-finance automation operated by Peter (“the tool”). The tool is used by one person only and is not offered as a public service.

1. Google user data accessed

The tool requests access to Google Drive solely to create, read, and edit the owner's private personal-finance spreadsheet and related files in the owner's own Drive. It does not request access to Gmail, Contacts, or any other user's data.

2. How the data is used

Data is used only to record transactions, calculate balances, produce the owner's reports, and maintain backups requested by the owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

3. Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored encrypted on a private server controlled by the owner. The finance data remains in the owner's Google Drive. Access is limited to the owner and the owner's private automation.

4. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own purposes. Requests may be processed by the AI model providers that operate the owner's assistant, solely to carry out the owner's instructions under their API terms. No data is made public.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is retained in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes it. The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
